How it works

Add to home screen first (Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen. Chrome: ⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install). Then it opens like an app and works offline. On Android you can also share a PDF straight to Vinterkort.

Routes. Make one route per Skyhost route, add all its PDFs, and use Edit to match the stop order. In a map, the arrows at the top go to the previous and next stop. When you walk into another stop's area, the bar at the bottom says "You're at #5…" with an Open button.

Calibrate once per PDF. Easiest: drag to fit. The PDF lies see-through on the satellite map: drag it into place, pinch to resize, twist to turn, Save. Or use points: The PDFs are pictures without map coordinates, so the app needs to learn where the picture sits in the world. Line up 2–3 spots far apart (opposite corners of the area work best). More points = more accurate. After 2 points, slide "PDF on top" to check the PDF lines up with the satellite picture.

"I'm standing here" works without internet: walk to a spot you can find on the PDF, put the top cross on it and tap the button. GPS is ±5 m, so pick spots far apart.

Area view (the map-sheet button) puts all calibrated stops within about 350 m on one satellite map, with your blue dot. Handy where you park once and walk between several stops. Tap a number to open that stop.

The blue dot shows where you are. The light circle around it is how sure the GPS is. If you're off the map, the bar at the bottom tells you how far and which way.

Google Drive. Tap Drive, sign in and pick a folder (for example H4). Its PDFs become a route, and tapping the folder again later only fetches new ones. Calibrations you save are written to vinterkort-calibrations.json in that folder, so they come back on a new phone and are reused when the same address shows up again. Maps marked "First guess: check it" were calibrated for you from the address: open them, slide "PDF on top" and nudge if needed.

Your PDFs stay on this phone and in your own Drive. Nothing is sent anywhere else. Deleting the app's site data or the home screen app removes the phone copy.
